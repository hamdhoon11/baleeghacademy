PortalSerif.ttf and PortalSerif-Bold.ttf are trimmed copies of GNU FreeSerif (GNU FreeFont), keeping only
Latin, Arabic and Thaana letters, made with: pyftsubset FreeSerif.ttf --unicodes=U+0020-007E,U+00A0-00FF,U+2000-206F,U+0600-06FF,U+0750-077F,U+0780-07BF,U+FB50-FDFF,U+FE70-FEFF,U+25CC
They are used only to draw Arabic and Dhivehi names on PDF receipts and invoices (receipts.py, rtltext.py).
